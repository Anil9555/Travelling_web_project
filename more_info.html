Taj Mahal

🎭 Cultural Significance

Symbol of Eternal Love: Built by Mughal Emperor Shah Jahan in memory of his wife Mumtaz Mahal.

Masterpiece of Mughal Art: Combines Persian, Islamic, and Indian architectural styles.

Marble Craftsmanship: Famous for intricate marble inlay work and detailed carvings.

UNESCO World Heritage Site: Recognized globally for its outstanding cultural value.

National Pride: One of the Seven Wonders of the World and India’s most iconic monument.

🏗️ Historical Highlights

Construction Period: Began in 1632 and completed in 1653.

Location: Situated on the banks of the Yamuna River in Agra, Uttar Pradesh.

Architect: Designed by Ustad Ahmad Lahauri.

Materials Used: Built with white marble brought from Rajasthan.

Workforce: Around 20,000 artisans and craftsmen contributed to its construction.

Architectural Layout: Features a grand dome, four minarets, and symmetrical Mughal gardens.
